Unallocated cash

Payments received without an invoice. They do not reduce any invoice's open amount until you allocate them. Allocate the whole payment to one invoice of the same customer; a payment larger than an invoice's open amount cannot go to that invoice (splitting is not supported). Every allocation, and every refused attempt, is audited.
